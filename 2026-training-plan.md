## Part 1: 8K Training (14 Weeks)

### Weekly Rhythm

- **Run 1 (Tue or Wed):** Easy run / intervals
- **Run 2 (Thu or Fri):** Workout run (easy in early phases, tempo later)
- **Run 3 (Sat or Sun):** Long run
- **Other days:** Cross-training, rest, or active recovery

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-3) — Feb 23 to Mar 15

All runs by time. Run/walk intervals. Pace should be conversational.

| Week | Run 1 | Run 2 | Run 3 (Long) | Total Time |
|------|-------|-------|---------------|------------|
| 1 (Feb 23) | 20 min: run 2 / walk 1 | 20 min: run 2 / walk 1 | 25 min: run 2 / walk 1 | ~65 min |
| 2 (Mar 2) | 22 min: run 3 / walk 1 | 22 min: run 3 / walk 1 | 28 min: run 3 / walk 1 | ~72 min |
| 3 (Mar 9) | 25 min: run 4 / walk 1 | 25 min: run 4 / walk 1 | 30 min: run 5 / walk 1 | ~80 min |

Drop the walk breaks sooner if you feel ready — your cross-training base may support it.

### Phase 2: Base Building (Weeks 4-8) — Mar 16 to Apr 19

Transition to continuous running. Track distance alongside time.

| Week | Run 1 (Easy) | Run 2 (Easy) | Run 3 (Long) | Weekly Miles |
|------|-------------|-------------|---------------|-------------|
| 4 (Mar 16) | 2 mi easy | 2 mi easy | 2.5 mi easy | ~6.5 |
| 5 (Mar 23) | 2.5 mi easy | 2.5 mi easy | 3 mi easy | ~8 |
| 6 (Mar 30) | 2.5 mi easy | 2.5 mi easy | 3.5 mi easy | ~8.5 |
| 7 (Apr 6) | **DOWN WEEK:** 2 mi | 2 mi | 2.5 mi | ~6.5 |
| 8 (Apr 13) | 3 mi easy | 3 mi easy | 4 mi easy | ~10 |

### Phase 3: Strength (Weeks 9-12) — Apr 20 to May 17

Run 2 becomes a workout with tempo intervals.

| Week | Run 1 (Easy) | Run 2 (Workout) | Run 3 (Long) | Weekly Miles |
|------|-------------|-----------------|---------------|-------------|
| 9 (Apr 20) | 3 mi easy | 3 mi w/ 4x2 min tempo | 4.5 mi easy | ~10.5 |
| 10 (Apr 27) | 3 mi easy | 3.5 mi w/ 2x8 min tempo | 5 mi easy | ~11.5 |
| 11 (May 4) | **DOWN WEEK:** 2.5 mi | 2.5 mi easy | 3.5 mi easy | ~8.5 |
| 12 (May 11) | 3 mi easy | 4 mi w/ 15 min tempo | 5.5 mi easy | ~12.5 |

**Tempo pace:** Comfortably hard. Short phrases, not full sentences. Roughly your target 8K race pace.

### Phase 4: Taper (Weeks 13-14) — May 18 to May 31

Reduce volume. Keep some intensity to stay sharp.

| Week | Run 1 (Easy) | Run 2 (Light Workout) | Run 3 | Weekly Miles |
|------|-------------|----------------------|-------|-------------|
| 13 (May 18) | 2.5 mi easy | 3 mi w/ 4x1 min at 8K pace | 3 mi easy | ~8.5 |
| 14 (May 25) | 2 mi easy | 2 mi w/ 4x30s strides | **RACE: 8K (May 31)** | ~4 + race |

## Effort Zones (The Talk Test)

| Zone | Feel | Can you talk? | When to use |
|------|------|--------------|-------------|
| Easy | Comfortable, sustainable | Full conversation | 80% of all running |
| Tempo | Comfortably hard | Short phrases only | Workout runs, Phase 3+ |
| Hard | Difficult, not sustainable | Single words | Race effort, rare intervals |

---

## Guardrails

1. **10% rule:** Never increase weekly mileage more than 10% week-over-week (down weeks excluded).
2. **80/20 split:** ~80% easy effort, ~20% tempo or harder.
3. **Pain protocol:** Muscle soreness is normal. Sharp pain (joints, shins) means stop and reassess.
4. **Cross-training:** Continue on non-running days. Avoid adding intensity the day before your long run.
5. **Recovery basics:** Sleep and hydration matter more than any single workout. Missing a run is fine.
6. **Down weeks:** Every 4th week, reduce volume ~30%. This is when your body actually adapts.
